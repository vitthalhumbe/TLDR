# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 2, 2026

Welcome to **TL;DR**. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our application. 

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you use our platform to study, track your progress, and interact with our AI tools.

**Account Registration Data:** When you create an account, we collect your authentication details, which may include your email address and password. 

**Study Materials & User Content:** To provide our core services, we collect the content you submit for ingestion, which includes uploaded PDF files, web URLs, and YouTube links.

**Interaction Data:** We log your interactions with our features, including your quiz scores, generated flashcards, chat history with our AI tutor, and responses during mock interviews.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and provide you with the features of the application.

**Service Delivery:** We process your uploaded materials to generate personalized study aids, such as summaries, quizzes, and mock interview scenarios.

**Personalization:** Your interaction data powers your user dashboard, allowing you to track your study progress over time.

**Customer Support:** We use your information to respond to your inquiries submitted through our contact page.

### 3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

To provide our AI capabilities and maintain robust infrastructure, we share specific data with trusted third-party service providers. **We do not sell your personal data to third parties.**

**Database & Authentication:** We use Supabase to securely manage user authentication and store user data.

**AI Processing:** To generate summaries, tutor responses, and quizzes, the text from your uploaded PDFs, URLs, and YouTube videos is sent to third-party Large Language Model (LLM) APIs. Specifically, we utilize Google Gemini and Groq to process this data. 

*Note regarding AI providers:* Please be aware that the study materials you submit are processed by these external AI services. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of Groq to understand their data retention practices regarding API usage.

### 4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

### 5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information and uploaded study materials only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. You may manage, delete, or modify your study materials directly through your dashboard. 

### 6. Your Data Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:
The right to access the personal information we hold about you.
The right to request the deletion of your account and associated data.
The right to correct any inaccurate information.

To exercise any of these rights, please reach out to us using the contact information below.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our technology, features, or legal requirements. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
